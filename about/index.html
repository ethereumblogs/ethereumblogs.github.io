About Crypto Structure Brief

Crypto Structure Brief is an independent newsroom covering crypto markets, trading infrastructure and on-chain capital flows. We examine who supplies liquidity, who takes risk and how incentives shape each development.

We prioritize primary documents, reproducible on-chain records and clearly attributed market data. We distinguish observed flows from inferred motives and disclose material conflicts. Commercial relationships do not determine coverage. We correct substantive errors transparently, identifying what changed and when.
